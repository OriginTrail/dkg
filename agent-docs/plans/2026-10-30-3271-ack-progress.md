Choose existing request-owned cancellation plus late-result fencing. Add a small round-lifetime helper owning the deadline, linked caller signal and abortable retry sleep; abort it on success/failure/timeout, clear timers/listeners, and forward its signal through ACKTransport to Messenger and the local self-ACK transport. Fence late send and identity-verification results before further work/status mutation. Existing transport and store physical-drain ownership remains authoritative; do not pretend cancellation stops an already-started identity RPC or an old remote core's handler. Preserve single-round concurrency,120-second timeout, candidate discovery and retry budgets.

Estimated cohesive scope: publisher collector/lifetime (~100 changed lines), agent transport/local forwarding (~30), regression and real-transport tests (~200), documentation/plan. Add aggregate cancelled/unresolved diagnostics only if necessary to establish physical cleanup; do not infer remote retirement from cancellation requests.
