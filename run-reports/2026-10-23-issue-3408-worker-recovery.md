# Testnet validation: issue 3408 worker recovery

## Executive Summary

Live validation passed the exercised recovery and concurrency scenarios: all 66 admitted shares succeeded and all 25 publications finalized, with exact canonical content and independent chain proofs. Current canary with #3395 and #3396 reproduced a remaining defect: automatic store discovery raced deferred subscription activation and stranded the required inventory lane. The fix restored the original share without re-admission, subscription repair, queue edits or changed timeouts. A fresh interruption then recovered on its first reclaim after real lease expiry. No additional KA identity, SWM head alias or VM publication was created by recovery. At-least-once gossip attempts retained the same operation and payload; remote internal apply counts and exactly-once delivery are not claimed. Two public-core quorum shortages healed through the original publication jobs' native retries. The claimed prepared wallet batch was reconciled and released after shutdown. The historical spontaneous stall's cause remains unproven.

## Known Limits

The campaign uses native macOS/Base Sepolia with Blazegraph, while the historical report used Gnosis. Deliberate SIGSTOP establishes replacement/recovery behavior, not the cause of the historical spontaneous worker stall. Public-core quorum shortages (an early baseline control and the fresh recovered asset's final publication) recovered through the original publication IDs' native retries; a read-only observer initially misclassified that retryable failed state, corrected without re-admission. Initial RPC balance qualification hit a public quota and was throttled without changing daemon budgets. At-least-once gossip attempts are distinguished from canonical protected state; remote internal apply counts were not available. An initial inventory-row check performed after VM publication found no active SWM row because VM promotion transfers that ownership; the fresh campaign measured the handoff before publication and proved exactly one row. A fresh mixed-workload harness initially selected an already-published control: the API correctly rejected it with `409 PUBLISH_NOT_FULL_SHARE` and admitted no publication. Two concurrent share responses were recovered from native persisted payloads before the harness continued; none were replayed. A phase-local chain-proof helper initially lacked the original graph-registration reference; reusing that same verified registration reference completed the read-only proof. A preliminary single-delivery observer assertion was corrected to distinguish identical at-least-once transport attempts from protected canonical effects; the failed observation is retained.
